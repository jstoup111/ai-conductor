## Judgement notes

The following were judged in this pass rather than checked mechanically.

- **Outcomes 4 and 6 (§4d cross-layer).** Outcome 4 preserves the verdict, routing, operator-authority, and delivery semantics of a *valid* verdict. Outcome 6 requires a missing or invalid output to be a mechanical fault. The plan changes only the second path: a rejected result reruns, then halts `needs-human` (Task 15), where a malformed table used to halt at admission. This is not a contradiction. The two outcomes govern disjoint inputs, and operator decision OD-1 plus adr-2026-08-25 remediable D2.1 authorize it.
- **Preserved-behavior sweep.** The following promise today's behavior: Story 6 negative 4 (kill switch), Story 6 happy 6 (no review marker), and Story 7 negative 6 (absent verdict at finish). No cited task adds an unconditioned side effect on those paths. Task 18 writes no marker. Task 20 yields no findings for an absent verdict. Task 17 reproduces the switch-off halt shape. Report rendering (Task 13) runs only after an accepted result, and none of these promises covers the report.
- **Architecture obligations (§4f).** Every `task` row's evidence asserts the decision's mechanism: identity stamping (Task 12), the single reader (Tasks 14 and 21), native-schema dispatch (Task 10), explicit limits (Task 8), and resolution through `parseAdrDecisions` (Task 3). Every `existing` and `no-change` row names behavior this feature does not alter, confirmed against the plan's task files.
- **Achievability (§4g).** Criteria whose Then-clause spans modules cite every task that owns a part:
  - Story 1 happy 1 cites 6 and 10 (projection contents plus the one prompt block).
  - Story 4 happy 5 cites 16, 1 and 6 (route, integer schema, projected decision ids).
  - Story 2 negative 5 cites 8 and 9 (no truncation, zero invocations).
