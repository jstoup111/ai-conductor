## Notes

Outcome 1 is delivered at the scope the operator confirmed during explore and conflict check. A preemption fires only when every slot is busy and the higher-band item also comes before the running feature in backlog order. With a free slot, the item dispatches normally, which also meets the outcome. Outcome 4 holds because a yield needs a strictly higher preemption rank (Story 1 negative paths).

Architecture obligations (§4f): each `task` disposition's cited `Done when` asserts the decision's behavior at its layer. D4's backward compatibility is asserted by task-2's older-child check and by task-2's protocol version check. D9's carve-out is asserted by task-6's no-marker checks. D10's child-only wiring is asserted by task-8's dependency wiring plus task-7's absent-dependency check. The adr-2026-08-05 `existing` rows cite the unchanged error path, which this feature's plan does not touch.

Achievability (§4g): every criterion row names the mechanism that delivers it (`preemptionRank` and `selectPreemptionVictim`, `parseExecutorMessage` and `runExecutorChild`, `requestYield`, the conductor's composed predicate and `stopAtOperatorParkBoundary` classification, `makeRunFeature`, `runDaemon`'s busy-pass trigger and release-only collection, the `daemon-cli.ts` wiring and `renderDaemonEvent`) along with its observable assertion. The closed result sets can represent every required scenario: the termination union (`operator-parked`, `preempted`), the feature status union (adds `preempted`) and the ledger outcome (`terminated`). The absence cases (no yield requested, no marker, no event) are asserted as absences. No criterion requires violating an approved decision.
