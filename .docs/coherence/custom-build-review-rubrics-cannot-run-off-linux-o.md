## Outcome, story, task, ADR and criterion mapping

Four staged intake outcomes (outcome 2 as amended on #2735 on 2026-09-24), seven stories, 17 tasks, two changed-ADR rows, and 43 criterion rows. Outcome quotes are the staged projection verbatim. No product FR layer applies: this is a technical-track spec with no PRD. Criterion quotes are exact completion-check text from a cited task's Done when block.

Consistency pass: every pair sharing a subject across layers was checked in both directions.
- Outcome 4 against tasks 10 to 12: capability probing runs only when an enabled custom member names a provider, so a built-in-only daemon start spawns nothing; the status section is additive.
- Story 7 against tasks 3, 4 and 13: the read-only option, digest and capability consult are set only on custom-policy lap members; task 17 pins the built-in-only invocations.
- Story 4 against the portable-policy D5.3 amendment: the feature checkout is outside the digest, which matches Story 4's third happy path.

Two corrections were made during this pass. Story 6's mixed-candidate criterion no longer claims a skip reason for a usage-limited candidate, which is invoked rather than skipped until #1492 lands. The portable-policy D5 obligation row now cites the adapter and capability tasks. No contradiction or achievability failure remains.
