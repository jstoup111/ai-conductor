## Notes

- **outcome-1** (gap, waived): the operator narrowed this outcome on 2026-10-26 to a kind-aware fence.
  An `audit` round never opens while another round is non-closed. A `revalidate` round still opens
  inside an audit round whose child hosts are settled and whose leaf host is `pending`, because
  `adr-2026-10-12` decisions 6 and 14 order the hosts as child hosts, then the queue, then the leaf.
  Story 2 carries the narrowed behavior. The waiver is in
  `.docs/coherence-waivers/routed-repair-opens-a-new-round-while-an-earlier-r.md`.
- **outcome-2** (gap, waived): the operator chose self-heal over halting on 2026-10-26. A feature
  holding two open rounds drains them on resume (Story 3). Only a state reconciliation cannot advance
  halts, with a `mechanical` HALT naming every open round, escalating to needs-human on the third
  identical stop (Stories 3 and 4). It never fails silently. The waiver is in the same file.
- **adr-2026-10-12-routed-whole-feature-repair**: all 18 decisions have a row in the plan's
  Architecture Obligation Coverage. The `task` rows (D6, D7, D13, D14, D16) quote the
  Done-when checks that assert the amended behavior. The `existing` rows (D15 `restageLeafGates`, D18
  `routingSettingOn`/`routingEligible`) were confirmed in `routing/routed-exit.ts` and
  `restack/stack-preflight.ts`. The `no-change` rows name decisions this feature does not touch.
