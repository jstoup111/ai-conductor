**Outcome notes.**
- outcome-1: Delivered at both DECIDE paths. Land refuses an ADR lacking a valid section (Story 3), the `/conduct` architecture review cannot complete for one (Story 4), and the parser checks presence and shape (Story 1). "Cannot reach APPROVED" is delivered as "an APPROVED ADR without a ledger cannot land or pass architecture review": a status line cannot be stopped from being typed, so the gates hold the boundary.
- outcome-2: A per-entry `APPROVED by operator YYYY-MM-DD` marker is recorded in the ADR. Operator-approved widening (2026-10-10): the marker is required on `inferred` as well as `unverified` load-bearing rows.
- outcome-3: `No load-bearing assumptions.` satisfies the parser (Story 1) and land (Story 3). A missing section is `missing-section` regardless of change size.
