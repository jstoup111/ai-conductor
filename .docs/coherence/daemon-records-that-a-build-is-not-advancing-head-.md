# Coherence: Daemon records that a build is not advancing HEAD but never acts on it (#2102)

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Tier:** M
**Track:** technical. The `fr` row class is omitted (no PRD); the six accepted technical stories carry the requirement layer.
**Outcome source:** the Desired-outcome bullets of jstoup111/ai-conductor#2102, staged by `compose worktree` and carried by the `.docs/intake/` marker.

## Outcome rationale

- outcome-1: Story 2 records the classified `build_active_stall` condition and renders it in `daemon.log`. Story 1 labels every progress line.
- outcome-2: Story 1 is the three-state classification.
- outcome-3: Story 2 is the minimum recorded condition (default `warn`), Story 3 is the opt-in end of the attempt, and Story 5 makes both configurable.
- outcome-4: Story 4 protects real work (per-project bound, movement clears, quiet never ends), and Story 5 is the per-project setting.
- outcome-5: Story 2 carries the condition on the existing event spine and the daemon log, with no other file.
