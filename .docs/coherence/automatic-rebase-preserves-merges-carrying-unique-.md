Outcome notes:

- outcome-1: The merge-only content survives as one flattened commit carrying the merge's exact tree delta, subject, and `Flattened-merge:` trailer, proven tree-equal to HEAD before any rebase (operator-approved mechanics, ADR D2 and D3); citations of the merge follow via amendment D10.
- outcome-2: Side-lineage commits are never replayed individually, on both the performRebase and autoresolve paths.
- outcome-3: `flatten_refused` halts before any rebase command, naming the merge sha, parents, flattened sha, and paths, with the recipe as the only recovery note.
