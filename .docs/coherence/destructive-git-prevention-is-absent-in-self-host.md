## Inputs and Judgment

This review compares the seven staged intake outcomes, the new APPROVED ADR (adr-2026-09-23-engine-git-guard-on-agent-path, D1–D10, with conflict-check amendments to D5 and D6), 12 accepted stories, 17 plan tasks and 50 extracted criteria. The plan and this table are rendered from one data source. Every criterion quote is checked as an exact substring of the cited task's `Done when` block.

- **Consistency:** preserved behaviour, meaning non-destructive git, other repositories, engine git and review allowlisting, is bounded by Tasks 4, 8 and 12. Every new side effect is conditional on an engine-prepared worktree, so unprepared and interactive dispatches are unchanged (Task 8).
- **Architecture tie-out (§4f):** every ADR decision is a `task` obligation, judged against the cited checks.
  - D1 (task-6): asset form and fail-closed write.
  - D2 (task-8, 9, 10): child-only `PATH` on both adapters and every dispatch shape; the daemon env is never mutated.
  - D3 (task-7, 8, 9): repair before launch, and no launch when the guard cannot be confirmed.
  - D4 (task-4): common-dir scoping.
  - D5 (task-1–4): the full refusal matrix, including the conflict-check reachability amendment.
  - D6 (task-5, 13): the refusal message, plus the claim-audit amendment.
  - D7 (task-12): engine and engine-CLI git unaffected.
  - D8 (task-14): heredoc fix.
  - D9 (task-15): `tdd` counterfactual.
  - D10 (task-16, 8, 9): executable proof across unit, adapter and live tiers. Its control-inventory recording is documentation and is the waived outcome-2 edit, not a plan obligation.
- **Cross-layer oscillation:** outcome-3 (safe forms work) against Stories 1–3 refusals is not an oscillation; each refused form's safe counterpart is asserted to pass in the same task. D5's reachability rule against Story 2 was reconciled at conflict-check.
- **Achievability:** each check names its mechanism (`GIT_GUARD_SCRIPT`, `writeGitGuard`, `ensureGitGuardForDispatch`, `withGitGuardPath`, `composeReviewLaunchMounts`, `auditEnvironmentBlockerClaims`, `auditShippedGithubInvocationBoundary`) and the asserting test.
- **Outcome 2 is waived:** the limitation inventory in `docs/reference/settings-and-hooks.md` is documentation, which stories and plans may not carry. It is routed to the documentation-maintenance step; see `.docs/coherence-waivers/destructive-git-prevention-is-absent-in-self-host.md`.
