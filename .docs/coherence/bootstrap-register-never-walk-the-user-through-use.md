## Inputs and Judgment

Consistency pass (§4d): no cross-layer contradiction. No new effect lacks a condition excluding the preserved scenario.

PRD↔stories tie-out (§4e): every FR-1..FR-14 is cited by a story whose scenarios exercise it, and no story cites an undeclared FR.

Architecture tie-out (§4f): adr-2026-08-28 D1, D2, D8 `existing` — verified in `config.ts` and `registry-cli.ts`; D3–D7 `no-change` — engine-internal verifier, evidence, and event decisions this feature does not touch; D9 `task` — task-1 and task-3 checks assert the flag is parsed into the dispatch and the rendered `test_suite.command` equals it with the flagless literal preserved, which is D9's obligation. adr-2026-08-09 decisions 1–3 `existing` — verified in `bin/install` and `cli.ts`; 4–5 `no-change`; decision 6 `task` — task-7 checks assert the `spec_owner` write, empty-value refusal, and unchanged rejection of every other non-`conductor` path, which is decision 6's obligation.
