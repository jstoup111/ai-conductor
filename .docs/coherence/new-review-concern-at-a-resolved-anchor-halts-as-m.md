## Consistency, tie-out, and achievability

- **Consistency (section 4d).** Every task check was compared with every criterion, negative paths first. Task 9 narrows the settled predicate only for a source that has an unresolved owner. Before this change no store could hold such a source, so preserved stores behave as before (Story 6). Tasks 7 and 13 add fields or text only when a rejection or declaration exists, which keeps the unchanged-output criteria in Stories 4 and 7. No contradiction or oscillation was found.
- **Architecture tie-out (section 4f).** Mixed-laps D5 and D6, prd-widening D2, and portable-policy D9, D11, and D12 map to tasks whose checks assert the amended rules. Every other decision is recorded as `no-change` with its reason. Portable-policy D8 holds because Task 4 compares only ids, dispositions, resolutions, and link outcomes, never rationale.
- **Achievability (section 4g).** Every criterion row matches a plan Coverage Check row that an independent coverage judge passed (claim 17 passed in an operator-approved fourth round). Each cited check names its mechanism: `parseBuildReviewCases`, `RemediationCaseStore.mutate`, `validateRemediationCaseGraph`, `reconcileRemediationCases`, `reduceBuildReviewAdjudication`, the coordinator's finalized-source set and `fail` path, or the findings CLI.
