# Coherence: Daemon startup dashboard readable at real repo scale

Source-Ref: jstoup111/ai-conductor#1332. Tier M, technical track (no PRD, so no `fr` rows).

Outcome mapping: outcome 1 is delivered by Story 1 (the first content line names what needs the operator) and Story 2 (actionable groups render first). Outcome 2 is Story 3: on the console, each inert group takes one count line, plus only the rows that still carry a remedy. On the issue's fixture that is 30 lines or fewer at 80 columns, against 102 today. Outcome 3 is Story 4. Free text and every chrome line are clipped to the terminal width (floor 40); the attention line wraps at its category separators rather than dropping a category; and slugs are never clipped, so no clipping makes two features identical or a slug uncopyable. This deliberately narrows the outcome's "every row fits": the operator chose 2026-10-24 that slug integrity wins, so on a terminal narrower than a slug line (a 50-character slug plus a 4-character prefix), that one line may wrap. The issue's own "slugs truncated to 50" observation is not a render truncation: slugs are capped at 50 when created. Outcome 4 is Story 5. That includes the production fallback path, where a failed origin-refresh pass is followed by a successful local pass whose blocked, waiting and gated specs render. Outcome 5 is Story 6. Outcome 6 is Story 7.

Consistency pass: no cross-layer contradiction found. Display order (Story 2) and bucket precedence (Story 6) are separate by design, as the amendments to `adr-2026-07-04-operator-park-marker` D5 and `adr-2026-07-03-owner-gate-gated-channel` D3 record. The full render keeps every existing row, so the rewritten criteria in `daemon-halt-reconciliation.md`, `operator-park-a-human-placed-halt-must-survive-the.md` and `daemon-reaps-a-feature-worktree-at-pr-open-before-.md` remain satisfied. The discovery-unknown render (Story 5 negative) is a defensive guard for a dashboard rendered with no completed pass. It no longer claims the daemon keeps starting after both passes fail; that path aborts before maintenance today, and changing it is out of scope. Plan review round 1 (PR-001, PR-002) drove the Story 5 and Story 4 amendments and Tasks 13–14.
