## Judgement notes

Consistency (4d): no counterpart opposes what it implements. The preserved-behavior sweep compared Story 8 and Story 2's no-template criterion with every new side effect: the pre-dispatch hook and capture hook act only for owning steps (Tasks 6 and 7), region masking and floor netting are identity functions with no regions or template (Tasks 11 and 3), the completion repair stays warn-only without a capture (Task 10), and Tasks 9 and 12 pin the unchanged operation sequence and body. Cross-layer check: the D7 amendment scopes verification to FINISH ready flips, matching Story 6; D4 and D5 match Stories 3 and 4. Ordering: Tasks 9 and 10 add region verification beside the release snapshot, which Task 14 removes only after Task 13 declares the release region, so this repository never builds without release-block preservation.

Architecture tie-out (4f): D1 and D2 are asserted by Task 2 config-load checks; D3 by Task 4 with Task 3 floor netting; D4 by Task 6; D5 by Task 7 over the Task 5 store; D6 by Tasks 8 and 9; D7 by Tasks 9 and 10; D8 by Task 11; D9 by Tasks 13 and 14. Achievability (4g): every criterion quote names its mechanism and asserts each Then outcome, including absence outcomes (no dispatch, no ready call, no capture, zero edits).
