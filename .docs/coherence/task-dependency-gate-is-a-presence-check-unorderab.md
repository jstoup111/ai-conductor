## Notes

- **outcome-6 (scope).** At the authoring-time strict points (land, and the conduct `plan` gate for new plans), an unparseable line is neither treated as valid nor guessed. It is rejected as `malformed-dependencies`, naming the task and the accepted forms (Story 1, Story 2, Story 3). Plans already on the default branch keep the presence-only check, by the operator-confirmed "new plans only" scope in `.docs/track/task-dependency-gate-is-a-presence-check-unorderab.md` (130 of 240 merged plans fail the strict parser). This scope decision is the reason `outcome-6` is recorded as covered and not as a gap.
- **outcome-5.** Conforming plans pass unchanged (Story 1 happy, Story 2 happy, Story 3 happy), merged plans keep today's eligibility (Story 4), and in-flight plans that exist at the merge-base never turn strict (Story 3).
