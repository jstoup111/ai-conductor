## Judgement Record

**Architecture obligations (§4f).** For `adr-2026-10-12-routed-whole-feature-repair`, D2 and D3 map to Task 1, whose checks require the task-bound placement and the judge receiving only unbound residue, which is exactly the amended D3 boundary. D6 maps to Task 4's `built` then `restacked` exit, which uses only existing legal host transitions. D7 maps to Tasks 4 and 5, which require the settle (no provider dispatch, no HALT, next host admitted) and the retained halt for rounds where no host did work. D10 maps to Task 3's check that no `routedRepairCredit` entry is written. D13 and D15 map to Task 4's ancestor and leaf-return checks. D16 maps to Task 2's outbox event check. The `existing` rows were confirmed by reading the cited symbols: `selectHost` in `routing/cannot-host.ts`, `resolveExecutionChild` in `routing/execution-child.ts`, `admitRoutedObligation` in `repair-restage.ts`, `acknowledgeQueueEntry` in `routing/revalidation-queue.ts`, `routingEligible` and `routingSettingOn` in `routing/route-types.ts`, and `classifyBuildProgress` and `shouldEscalateKickback` in `kickback-escalation.ts`. D5 and D12 are `no-change`, because a settled host runs no builder and no `build_review`. For `adr-2026-07-13-kickback-build-no-op-escalation`, D1 maps to Tasks 4 and 5 (the narrowed halt). D2 and D3 are untouched N=1 escalation machinery.

**Achievability (§4g).** Each criterion is delivered by a named mechanism:
- `routeAdmittedRound`'s task-bound branch for Story 1.
- `settleDerivedNoOpHost` for the settle-or-halt decision and the no-credit outcome.
- `enterRoutedHostRegion`'s `derived-already-complete` arm feeding `exitRoutedHostAndReconcile` for the conductor-level settle, restack, leaf return and halt.
- reconciliation plus the same arm for `halt clear` recovery.
- `resolveHaltRetention` for the retained halt.

The host-state set is closed. Every required outcome is a legal value: `done` (no-commit exit), `restacked` (entry committed), `admitted` (halt leaves the host unchanged), and `closed` (round close). Story 2 negative 4 (crash mid-settle) accepts either `done` or `restacked`, matching the two legal exits from `repairing`. No criterion depends on an outside-diff commit. Every disposition is `diff-local`.

**Accepted DECIDE change outside this feature's stories.** The no-op guard criterion in Story 1 of `.docs/stories/a-routed-fix-cannot-be-built-charged-and-reviewed-.md` was narrowed in place, so it no longer contradicts Story 2 here. Task 4's halt check and Task 5's single-host and all-no-op checks keep it true.
