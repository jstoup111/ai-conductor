# Coherence: Lease-guarded intake claim recovers stranded envelopes

Source-Ref: jstoup111/ai-conductor#2733. Tier M, technical track (no PRD, so no `fr` rows).

Outcome mapping: outcome-1 (an interrupted claim never leaves pending intake unclaimable) is delivered by Story 1 (recovery at the next claim, including a dead claimer's lease) and Story 3 (pending-only scope). Outcome-2 (no double handout of a live claim) is delivered by Story 2 (overlapping claims are serialized on the claim lease, so recovery never touches a live walk's envelopes) and Story 3 (a ledger-`claimed` entry is never recovered by reconciliation); the pre-existing bounded duplicate-processing window of `adr-2026-07-22-heartbeat-lease-deferred` is unchanged, as recorded in ADR-011 decision 6. Outcome-5 (pre-fix strands self-heal) is delivered by Story 1. Outcome-3 (recovery verbs accept the mismatch) and outcome-4 (mismatch count in queue health) are gaps waived by the operator's minimal scope boundary — see `.docs/coherence-waivers/interrupted-intake-claim-still-strands-high-priori.md`.

Consistency pass: no cross-layer contradiction. Reconciliation runs only under the claim lease (Task 4) and releases only pending-ledger strands (Tasks 3, 7), which keeps Story 2's no-double-walk guarantee and Story 3's scope compatible; `claimUnblocked`, the delivery guard and stale-claim reaping are unchanged (Task 7). All criterion rows were judged by an independent coverage judge before landing. The 20 criterion rows here are identical to the plan's `## Coverage Check` rows, which the judge accepted in round 1 (20 of 20), so they need no second judgement.

Gap ids (canonical form): `outcome-3` and `outcome-4`, both waived. Outcome quote cells carry only the staged bullet; the gap rationale lives here and in the waiver.

Achievability (§4g): each cited check names its mechanism (`withIntakeClaimLease`, `reconcileStrandedClaims`, `listClaimed`, the `claim` dispatch of `dispatchEngineer`) and asserts the Then-outcomes at the `compose claim` layer the criteria name. Task 5's lease-absent check holds because `conduct-state-lease.ts` releases by removing the lease directory (verified). Architecture tie-out (§4f): ADR-011 decision 6 is delivered by task-4 (reconcile under the lease, then the unchanged walk) and task-5 (bounded wait and in-progress refusal); decision 4 by task-1 (no `daemon-lock.ts` import, rename primitive untouched); decision 3 by task-2 (port type unchanged). The banding ADR's decisions 1-4 impose no change, because `claimUnblocked` ordering, band resolution, fail-open and vocabulary are untouched. Preserved-behavior sweep: Task 4's new stderr line appears only when N > 0, matching Story 1's no-strand negative; recovery writes no ledger bytes (Task 3), matching Story 3's read-only criterion; stale-claim reaping stays on the unchanged delivery-guard path (Task 7). No `fail` rows.
