## Consistency and Achievability

- **§4d criterion-conflict sweep:** run by a fresh subagent over the plan, stories, and ADR
  decisions. Round 1 found one conflict: Task 5's mergeable reconcile (required by
  adr-2026-10-09-shipped-pr-readiness-verdict D3) could remove a pre-existing `mergeable` label on a
  `no-checks` PR, contradicting Story 3's "no GitHub operation other than the existing label add".
  Resolved in DECIDE by narrowing that Story 3 criterion to allow the existing reconcile and making
  Task 6 assert it with a `mergeable`-labelled fixture; two criteria were also made explicitly
  non-draft. Round 2 over the revised plan: no conflicts. Its one unverifiable risk — whether zero
  check runs is distinguishable from pending — was confirmed in source: `classifyChecksOutcome` in
  `pr-labels.ts` returns `none` for an empty rollup, distinct from `pending` and `green`.
- **Preserved-behaviour sweep:** Story 1's "exactly as before" criteria (`ready` → `mergeable`,
  `ci-pending` → `mergeable` removed, MERGED/CLOSED/NOTFOUND handling) are pinned by Task 5; the new
  side effects on that path (watch-entry writes, `shipped_pr_readiness` emission, readiness label)
  are each conditioned on an OPEN, classified entry and never touch the lifecycle branches.
- **§4g achievability:** every criterion's cited checks name the mechanism (`classifyShippedReadiness`,
  `routeShippedReadiness`, `maybeClearReadinessLabel`, the sweep's re-read and head tracking, the
  `daemon-cli.ts` `onEvent` binding, the `daemon-observe-cli.ts` reader) and assert the
  Then-outcome at the criterion's layer. The closed verdict set represents every required scenario,
  including the absence outcomes (`ci-pending` and `indeterminate` waits, no label call, no event).
  No `fail` or `gap` rows.
