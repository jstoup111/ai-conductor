# Coherence: Daemon session command compatibility and visibility

Source-Ref: jstoup111/ai-conductor#2709. Tier M, technical track (no PRD, so no `fr` rows).

Outcome mapping: outcome 1 (pre-merge check) is delivered by Story 1 with the FINISH (Story 2) and bootstrap (Story 3) instruction corrections; outcome 2 (interactive-only exemption) by Story 1 context classification and Story 3 operator bootstrap; outcome 3 (refusal on spine and daemon log) by Stories 4, 5 and 7; outcome 4 (raw gh write observability) by Stories 4, 6 and 7 within the operator-approved bounded coverage of ADR D7 (absolute binaries, replaced PATH and custom HTTP clients are explicitly outside monitoring coverage).
