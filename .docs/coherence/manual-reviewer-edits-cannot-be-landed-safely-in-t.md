## Outcome Notes

- **outcome-1:** `stack land` opens the round, switches the worktree and records the declaration, and `--abandon` resets the branch (Stories 1 and 9). Nobody edits a branch or engine state by hand. `stack owners` and the shipped skill decide which child owns the change (Stories 12 and 13). Scope covers unpublished stacks only (operator, 2026-10-24).
- **outcome-2:** The pre-commit guard refuses commits on every branch but the host, in every round kind, with or without a `Task:` trailer (Story 4). The branch-tip check catches `--no-verify` commits and commits from other checkouts (Story 5).
- **outcome-3:** An undeclared landing halts and is never dispatched, restacked or re-validated (Story 6). Raw closed-child commits made outside a landing halt and are carried by a landing, never closed over (Story 7, operator decision 2026-10-24).
- **outcome-4:** After `--done`, the child re-validates from `acceptance_specs` and leaves through the shipped exit restack. The re-validation queue re-judges every moved closed child above it, and the leaf gates re-run (Story 8). Budgets and reroutes are as Story 11 says. The queue is selective, which is #2944's guarantee.
- **outcome-5:** Story 10: a closed-child rewind opens a declared operator round and runs the same exit, queue and leaf return.
- **outcome-6:** The feature dispatch lease excludes dispatch and operator commands (Story 2). The landing takes a token-owned park (Story 3). The open predicate refuses while any round, queue entry or restack journal is open (Story 1).
- **outcome-7:** Story 15: flat features and routing-off stacks run today's code, and the N=1 golden guard is extended to the lease.
