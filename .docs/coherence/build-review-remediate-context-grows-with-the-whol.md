## Notes

- **outcome-3.** Its first clause, a ceiling every provider transport can deliver, is met by existing, verified code rather than by a story: Claude print mode, Codex and Pi all deliver the prompt over stdin (`claude-provider.ts`, `codex-provider.ts`, `pi-provider.ts`), and the engine ceiling stays the existing 256 KiB `maxSerializedBytes`. Story 1 keeps that ceiling as a named `serialized-byte-overflow` stop, and Story 4 delivers the named, operator-readable failure.

## Judgement Record

All judges were fresh subagents on the session default model (Tier M).

- **Coverage (plan §7a).** Round 1: 26 of 30; claims 11, 13, 28 and 29 were tightened. Round 2: 4 of 4. Round 3, after the coherence fixes: 20 of 20.
- **Coherence (§4f, §4g and the cross-layer sweep), round 1.** Three blocking findings and five advisory ones.
  - Persisted case effects carry no tasks, so open-case admitted ids are now read from the published work order, with a coordinator-level check.

  - The validator returns at its first defect, so `classifyIndexOnlyAdmission` reports the re-validation's own defect (`missing-source`), with a coordinator-level halt check.
  - Both context assemblies now receive the scoped contract.
  - Advisory: mixed-laps D3 is now a task row; portable-policy D9 gained a same-date cross-reference note for the expansion exception.
- **Coherence, round 2.** All four round-1 blocking findings were confirmed resolved, and 30 of 30 criterion rows were clean. One new §4f finding was fixed: the portable-policy D9 row now cites tasks 1, 4 and 6, which together assert the discard, the single expansion round and the unchanged stops. The D8 note and the plan now say pointer-named tasks *of dispatched findings*. Accepted advisory: the pre-dispatch assembly selects from the sources before the settled filter, so it may carry a few more bodies than the fresh assembly; no criterion depends on this.
