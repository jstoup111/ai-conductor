## Outcome notes

- **outcome-1.** The resolution is made on a scratch branch named in the halt and recorded with `stack restack-resolve`, not by committing on the child's own branch. That follows the operator-approved approach B (`adr-2026-10-25-operator-recorded-restack-conflict-resolution`, Option B, 2026-10-25). The outcome's tested substance is covered by story-2 and story-3: the next dispatch accepts the operator's resolution, the restack applies, and the resolved commits are not replayed again.
- **outcome-4.** The halt text half is story-1 (task-3). The runbook half is documentation, which the plan and stories skills exclude by rule (ADR decision 10). It is delivered by the pipeline's documentation step, not by a plan task.
- **outcome-5.** No engine path moves a child branch to adopt or discard operator work (ADR decisions 3–5). A recorded resolution survives every aborted restack (story-5, S5.3), and the halt names where the resolution lives (story-1).
