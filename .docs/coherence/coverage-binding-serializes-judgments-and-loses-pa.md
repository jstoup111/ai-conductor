## Consistency and achievability notes

- §4d: no cross-layer contradiction. Outcome-1 (faster) vs D12 sequential batches: satisfied by ⌈N/8⌉ sessions, not by concurrency; neither re-breaks the other. Outcome-5 (a failed judgment keeps unrelated verdicts) vs task-7 (whole batch rejected): a rejected batch discards only its own unrecorded verdicts, and earlier batches stay — compatible with D13.
- §4f: D5's unchanged obligations (fresh session, `resume: false`, digest identity) are asserted by task-5; D6 by task-8; D8 by task-4; D9 by task-6; D12–D15 by the cited tasks' Done-when checks. D1–D3, D10, D11 are land-time contracts this BUILD-phase change does not touch; D4 and D7 are verified existing code (`steps.ts` step entry; `runCoverageBinding` disabled branch).
- §4g: Story 2 negative 6 relies on the existing retry classifier treating a `refusal`-less failure as ordinary (shipped by #2371); task-7 asserts the absence of `refusal`, which is the input that classifier keys on. Story 3 negative 1 relies on the existing conductor re-dispatching an incomplete step; task-1 asserts the completion derivation reports incomplete for `partial`.
