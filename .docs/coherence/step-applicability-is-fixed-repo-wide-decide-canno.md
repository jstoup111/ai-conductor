**Track:** product
**Outcome source:** the Desired-outcome bullets of jstoup111/ai-conductor#1789, staged in `.pipeline/intake-outcomes.md` and carried by the `.docs/intake/` marker.

## Outcome rationale

- outcome-1: Story 3 records the declaration with its reason; Story 6 honors it for that feature only.
- outcome-2: Story 9 records a distinct inapplicable cause, event, dashboard icon, and daemon-status line.
- outcome-3: Story 1 makes the capability project-only and default off; Story 2 proves an unset repository dispatches exactly as before.
- outcome-4: Story 7 honors only operator-merged base declarations (autonomous branches, invalid markers, and interactive runs are ignored); Story 10 records author, committer, and sha.
- outcome-5: Story 4 fixes the declarable set to acceptance_specs and manual_test with the correctness core never declarable; Story 8 refuses declarations for steps already started.
