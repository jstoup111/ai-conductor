## Achievability

Every criterion row was walked against its cited `Done when` checks and the amended ADR decisions
7, 9 and 13. Each check names the producing mechanism (`readGateVerdictOverlay`'s round mask,
`routedRoundForActiveChild`, `stopParkedForRouting`, `continueRoutedHostExit`, or #3445's
`recordRoundStall`) and asserts the Then-clause at the criterion's layer. No check requires behavior
an approved decision forbids, so no row is `fail`.

One assumption remains, and it is carried as architecture-review Condition 1, not as a gap. The
Story 4 escalation rows rely on #3445's `recordRoundStall` landing with the planned identity
(deep-equal `rounds`) and the planned reset events (`routed_repair_phase`, `remediation_routed`).
