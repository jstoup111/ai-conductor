## Architecture decision judgement (§4f)

- `adr-2026-11-07-child-projected-dispatch-otel`:
  - **D1** (one projector per dispatch) is asserted by Task 11. Its accepted pair counts each step, run, dispatch and shipment once. Its older-child and non-accepting pairs export through the supervisor with no child-written span batch.
  - **D2** (two-sided negotiation) is asserted by Task 6's acceptance-only-with-capability-and-provider checks, Task 7's no-acceptance-no-projection check, and Task 10's OTel-disabled check.
  - **D3** (child spans, store-only spool, same exporters otherwise) is asserted by Task 4 (writer runtime with no lease or drainer) and Task 5 (`exporter: file` output parity).
  - **D4** (flush before outcome) is asserted by Task 7 on the success and error paths, and by Task 11's ordering of `feature_dispatch_ended` after the outcome.
  - **D5** (child `MetricsListener` over a synchronous brokered meter; observable kinds throw) is asserted by Task 2.
  - **D6** (host materializes on the one meter, shares storage, stamps identity, discards after close) is asserted by Task 3, with the shared-stream, identity-override and post-close checks.
  - **D7** (skip recording, keep terminal bookkeeping) is asserted by Task 9.
  - **D8** (unknown types keep their spine treatment and lose no OTel; child-local types projected by neither process) is asserted by Task 8's `knownEventTypes` check, and by Task 11's skewed-pair export and `otel: false` checks.
  - **D9** is a constraint and is correctly `no-change`. Task 1 types the traffic as an executor protocol message, and no task adds an event variant or ledger.
- `adr-2026-10-15-per-dispatch-engine-pinned-executor`:
  - **D3** (versioned protocol) is asserted by Task 1's unchanged version and backward-compatible parsing. **D4** (amended) is asserted by Task 8: no supervisor visualizer for an accepted attachment, and the unknown-type drop is kept. **D1** and **D2** are correctly `existing`: the child-only executor and the WorkOrder pin are #3333's baseline. **D5–D10** impose no change here (lifecycle binding, GC pins, restart policy, cadence rebuild, status, and that ADR's own delivery shape).
- `adr-014-otel-observability-exporter`:
  - **D4** is asserted by Task 2's synchronous-record check. **D5** is asserted by Task 5's bounded fallback. **D7** (amended) is asserted by Task 10's host bound to the one `MeterProvider`. **D8** is asserted by Task 3's identity stamping. **D13** is asserted by Task 5's `otel.attributes` check. **D15** and **D16** are asserted by Task 4.
  - **D1–D3**, **D6**, **D10**, **D12**, **D14** and **D18–D20** are correctly `existing`. The child reuses `wireOtelVisualizer`, `MetricsListener` and `MetricsRecorder` unchanged under the same merged config (`executor-child-runtime.ts` `loadMergedConfig`). **D9**, **D11** and **D17** are correctly `no-change`: daemon-level signals, the no-new-event-type constraint, and spool health all stay supervisor-side.

## Achievability (§4g)

Every criterion row was checked against its cited `Done when` checks, and each quote is verbatim from a cited task. The skewed-pair rows are achievable because Task 11's fixture narrows the supervisor's registry through the injectable `knownEventTypes` (Task 8) and listener types (Task 10), while the child keeps its full registry. A passing check therefore observes a type that the supervisor's projection cannot handle. The run-close rows are representable: the listener's existing `terminal` set expresses both "child closed the run" and "fallback closes it" (Task 9). No cited check could deliver its criterion only by violating an approved decision. In particular, Story 1's child spool writes respect ADR-014 D16, because Task 4 requires no lease and no drainer.
