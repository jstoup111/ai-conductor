## Semantic judgement

Coverage was evaluated from the combined Done-when checks of every cited task, not from the quote alone. Boundary producers and consumers are both cited where neither suffices on its own.

- Timing (Story 1): producer admission/settlement (11–14), persisted freeze (3), projection (7/9) and authoritative finalization (17) jointly deliver member-local duration. A late gate verdict cannot inflate elapsed time or create an early successful terminal. Width-one stays on the serial integration; non-admitted work never starts.
- Attribution (Story 2): explicit identity (1), invocation-local propagation (5), context-aware projections (7/9), configured wiring (14), forwarding (16), and late-terminal isolation (15) collectively distinguish the same-name and cross-feature cases. Missing data stays absent; correlation is excluded from metric Resource and point labels.
- Retries (Story 3): actual policy events (11/12/13), scoped attempt metadata (5), deduplication (6), and retry projection (8/9) distinguish logical execution, invoked attempts and policy retries. Rate-limit waits do not consume policy budget, and real exhaustion stays failed.
- Terminals (Story 4): the lifecycle owner (2), refusal projections (8/10), actual shutdown wiring (15), authoritative classification (17) and honest ledger reader (4) cover refusal, failure, racing shutdown and irrecoverable loss without inventing outcomes.
- Authority (Story 5): the group integration and negative-outcome finalization (13/14/17) preserve existing state/gate ownership. Failure-isolated projection (10/12/16/17) neither grants gate success nor swallows persistence failure.
- Compatibility (Story 6): the resolver's distinct legacy namespace (1), persisted interval handling (3), reader (4), dispatch suppression (6), metric projection (7) and forwarding (16) jointly retain legacy behavior while preventing correlated records from stealing old/new matches. Overlap remains a union.
- Regression prevention (Story 7): required observers (11) and real registry-derived integration fixtures (13/14, with serial baseline 12) exercise actual membership, not a hand-maintained event allowlist. The counterfactual is a dispatched member without lifecycle output and must fail behavior assertions.
