**Plan:** .docs/plans/pipeline-batch-evaluators-remain-an-unenforced-sec.md
**Stories:** .docs/stories/pipeline-batch-evaluators-remain-an-unenforced-sec.md
**Track:** technical, so there are no `fr` rows. Outcomes come from the staged intake projection for jstoup111/ai-conductor#3056 (`.pipeline/intake-outcomes.md`). Tier L.

Consistency pass: an independent subagent ran the criterion-conflict sweep over the final plan, covering every task against every criterion and every subject ADR decision, plus task-versus-task and preserved-behavior sweeps. It found no contradiction and no oscillation. It flagged three risks it could not verify from the artifacts alone, and each was then checked against source. `dispatchBuildReviewAccept` is defined in `build-review-cli.ts`, not in the deleted `closeout-cli.ts`. `/code-review`'s own model selection carries the risk-domain escalation (`security boundaries, auth, or money`). `agents/evaluator.md` contains no batch- or pipeline-evaluator gate claim for task 17's scan to match.

Achievability pass: each criterion's cited `Done when` checks name the mechanism that produces its Then-clause. Examples are the default-enabled map in `resolved-config.ts` and build_review classification in `step-runners.ts`, `CloseoutEventTail.pollOnce` and its admitted-kind list, the CLI's unknown-command fallthrough in `index.ts`, `SESSION_SANCTIONED_SUBCOMMANDS`, the OTel recorder and span manager, the rollup types, the `build-tail` command entry, and the shell contract predicates. Every closed set the criteria depend on can represent each required outcome: the rollup's `measured | partial | unavailable` states, and `pipeline_tail_diagnostic.reason` extended with `unadmitted-type`. The independent coverage judgement (plan §7a) passed all 51 criterion rows, and these rows are identical to the plan's `## Coverage Check`.
