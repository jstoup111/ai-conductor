# Coherence: Machine-Authored and Machine-Verified Issue Dependency Edges

Source-Ref: jstoup111/ai-conductor#536. Tier M, product track.

Outcome mapping. Outcome 1 (linking at creation time) is delivered by Stories 1–4. Structured-field linking had already shipped before this spec (#2831), and Story 1 covers the structured field and prose together. Linking is limited to issues the harness is authorized to modify, per adr-2026-09-11-github-operation-ownership; the drift report surfaces the rest. Outcome 2 (DECIDE proposes edges, the operator confirms, nothing silently applied) is Stories 5–9. Outcome 3 (a drift report, never auto-mutated) is Stories 10–13. Outcome 3's "links to closed issues" is interpreted as blockers closed as not planned; this was operator-approved in the PRD (FR-14b and FR-18). Outcome 4 (ambiguous prose creates no edge) is Story 3.

Consistency pass. A fresh-subagent sweep (plan §7b, run three rounds, then again over the final plan) found no remaining cross-layer contradiction. Three earlier findings were resolved in this DECIDE pass:
- the drift event gained a status, so a failed listing is never read as clean;
- Story 2's title-only criterion was narrowed to match "prose is the declaration of record";
- offline land, the advisory overlap scan, and the migration non-goal were reconciled through ADR decision 6, ADR decision 3, and amendment notes on the two older ADRs.

Preserved behavior is checked as follows:
- Story 7: non-intake land makes no dependency reads and emits no event (Task 11).
- The advisory overlap-scan and intake-file callers keep their behaviour (Task 9 Steps).
- The migration command stays standalone (prose-to-link migration ADR D4).
