## Judgement Record

**Architecture obligations (§4f).** The rows for `adr-2026-10-12-routed-whole-feature-repair` were judged as follows.

- D1 maps to Task 2, which requires a taskless obligation to rewind into a dispatched BUILD carrying its retry hint, as decision 1 states.
- D3 maps to Tasks 7 and 8. They require the single routing call to receive the FAIL rows and per-position paths, and placement to follow kept paths or fall back to the leaf. That is the amended decision 3 boundary, and Task 9 keeps the mixed-round placement unchanged.
- D4 maps to Task 8's owner-1, host-4 `prerequisite-paths` check, so the host moves up only on evidence.
- D6 maps to Task 11's single transaction (`update-record` plus `delete-marker`), the amended `closed` writer.
- D7 maps to Task 1's `entered`/`repairing` check, the amended guard, and to Task 12's CLI writer behavior: lease and reason checked before any write.
- D13 maps to Task 3's ancestor check. D15 and D16 map to Task 13's leaf-gate dispatch and its events.jsonl line.

The `existing` rows were confirmed by reading each cited symbol: `routeAdmittedRound` in `routing/round-router.ts`, `resolveExecutionChild` in `routing/execution-child.ts`, `chargeTasklessAtAdmission` in `routing/round-receipt.ts`, `admitRoutedObligation` in `repair-restage.ts`, `exitRoutedHostAndReconcile` in `conductor.ts`, `routingEligible` and `routingSettingOn` in `routing/route-types.ts`, and `classifyBuildProgress` and `shouldEscalateKickback` in `kickback-escalation.ts`. D5 and D12 are `no-change`, because no builder prerequisite claim or routed review base changes.

For `adr-2026-07-13-kickback-build-no-op-escalation`, D1 maps to Tasks 1 and 4: the vacuous halt is removed for taskless hosts and kept for task-bound ones. D2 and D3 are untouched escalation machinery.

**Achievability (§4g).** Each criterion is delivered by a named mechanism:

- `hostCarriesTasklessObligation` inside `enterRoutedHostRegion` handles Story 1 entry. The predicate is structural (a hosted item with no rem task and no `taskIds`) because the custom-step admission records no `retryHint`. Task 2's custom-step check drives the real `admitRoutedCustomStepKickback` path, so the production record shape reaches the guard.
- `changedPathsByPosition`, the v2 `runResidueRoutingCall`, and the leaf-tip filter feeding `selectHost` inside `admitRoutedTasklessKickback` handle Story 2.
- The `leaf-return` result of `checkRoundSupersession`, `supersedeRound`'s one-transaction write, and `dispatchStackRoundsCommand` handle Story 3.
- The round-kind switch in `runStackCommitGuard` handles Story 4.

The closed host-state set represents every required outcome: `repairing` (dispatched), `restacked` and `done` (the two exits), `admitted` (refusal leaves the host unchanged), and `closed` (round close). The event's closed `disposition` set (`superseded`, `leaf-return`) represents both close shapes. Story 3 happy 5 depends on approved assumption A1 (the leaf resumes from its kickback-set state once no round or marker remains); Task 13 owns that proof. No criterion depends on an outside-diff commit, so every disposition is `diff-local`.
