**ADR rows:** none — the current change set adds or modifies no `.docs/decisions/adr-*.md` file.

## Outcome rationale

- outcome-1: Story 1 labels an adjudicable build_review FAIL as provisional and renders one final-verdict line per adjudicated lap.
- outcome-2: Story 2 names findings by title on the failure and suppressed-finding lines; Story 3 keeps UUID-shaped tokens out of the default log.
- outcome-3: Story 4 logs retention once per disposition change across all three retention checks.
- outcome-4: Story 5 renders containment, fingerprint and session-policy lines once per dispatch, makes routine provider lifecycle lines verbose-only, and stops the routine session-replacement notice.
- outcome-5: Story 6 collapses forwarded multi-line, git and JSON output to one line naming where the full text is kept.
- outcome-6: Story 7 requires a next-action suffix on every warning and halt line.
