## Judgement notes

- **outcome-1** is covered by Story 1 (host precedence from the flag, then the `explore` step's `llm_provider`, then the default) and Story 2 (per-host argv). The launcher resolves the `explore` step's selection, which is the `llm_provider` that governs DECIDE (ADR D10).
- **outcome-2** is a deliberate `gap`, waived in `.docs/coherence-waivers/compose-launcher-honors-llm-provider-for-codex.md`. This feature delivers the missing entry point: the launcher starts Codex on `$composer`. The operator-confirmed scope boundary excludes Codex sandbox and permission tuning, so authoring, `land`, and `handoff` inside a Codex session are not asserted end to end.
- **outcome-3** is covered by Story 2 (a host without `interactiveLaunch` is refused naming the provider, the capability, and #1007) and Story 3 (a missing executable is named with its override variable and the in-session alternative, with no silent fallback).
- **adr-2026-09-24-built-in-provider-catalog-and-boot-discovery**: D9–D11 are delivered by tasks 1, 2, 4, 6, and 7. D1 is honored by task 4's structural-test check and D2 by task 1's refusal check. D3–D8 impose no change on this feature: the launcher runs no boot discovery, adds no provider, and leaves Pi's adapter and capabilities untouched.
- **Consistency pass.** Story 4 exits 0 before config load, while Stories 1 and 2 require a config-driven launch. These do not conflict: they apply only when no session marker is set, and task 7's last check asserts exactly that. Task 6's "never with the claude executable" and Story 1's "no fallback" agree. Task 4's argv checks and Story 2's `plan`-coercion negative agree, because task 1 moves the coercion into the claude descriptor.
