## Judgement notes

These are the three rows whose verdict rests on a judgement a reader should be able to check rather than re-derive.

**outcome-4.** The staged bullet asks that the stories skill's authored output satisfy the extractor by construction. That is already true at this HEAD: `skills/stories/SKILL.md` shows headed `#### Happy Path` and `#### Negative Paths` sections with single-line Given/When/Then bullets, which is exactly the shape the extractor reads, and the skill additionally forbids authored criterion ids. The outcome therefore needs no new authoring work; what this feature adds is that any drift away from that shape now fails at land instead of at `acceptance_specs`. It is mapped to story-1 because story-1 is where that enforcement and its required-shape message live. Verified by reading the skill template, 95%.

**criterion row for Story 4 happy 2 (task-9).** The cited check asserts that the `acceptance_specs` required-evidence derivation calls no readability predicate. Task 1 does change what `extractAuthoritativeStoryCriteria` returns for a hard-wrapped bullet, and that function feeds this derivation, so a reader may ask whether the criterion still holds. It does: the criterion promises only that *the readability predicate* adds no requirement. The derivation change is a separate obligation that `adr-2026-09-23-one-owner-for-accepted-story-readability` decision 3 exempts explicitly, on the grounds that it changes derivation rather than adding a requirement and corrects BUILD-side routing that is already wrong.

**criterion row for Story 1 negative 3 (task-4), the Small-tier case.** `adr-2026-07-21-s-tier-pipeline-knobs` D1 states that smallness is expressed entirely through existing resolution knobs with "no new land primitive", and this criterion engages a new land rung at tier S. That ADR is not in this spec's change set, so it is outside the achievability constraint sweep, and the rung is tier-agnostic rather than S-specific — it applies identically at S, M, and L, as the existing approval and plan-reference rungs already do. The reading is recorded in `.docs/conflicts/` rather than settled here; if the operator reads the clause as forbidding any new land rung at S, this criterion is the one to amend.
