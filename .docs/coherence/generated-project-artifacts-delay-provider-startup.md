## Gap notes

Gap ids outcome-1, outcome-2, outcome-3 and outcome-4 are the declaration-mechanism outcomes of #1219. They are deliberately undelivered: the pre-stories architecture review found the mechanism contradicts approved `adr-2026-08-17-structural-live-checkout-containment` decision 4 and that its latency premise is falsified by measurement, and the operator selected resolution R-1 (ship the duration signal, defer the declaration mechanism) on 2026-09-21. No story cites them, by design. They are waived in the coherence waiver for this stem, and #1219 stays open for them.

## Consistency and achievability notes

- **outcome-6 is covered by story-3, not vacuously.** With no declaration mechanism in this spec, every project is a project "without a declaration"; Story 3 pins the manifests, exclusion sets, and the fail-closed drift halt as unchanged, which is the behaviour the outcome protects.
- **Preserved-behaviour sweep.** Story 3 promises unchanged guard behaviour; the only new side effect on that path is Task 6's emit. It is bounded to the self-host path after a completed fingerprint (Task 7, first two checks), goes through `ConductorEventEmitter.emit`, which isolates handler errors (Task 7, third check), and `verifyLiveBoundary` compares `surface.manifest` only (Task 3, fourth check). No task check introduces an effect on the manifest, the exclusion sets, or the verification verdict.
- **Cross-layer oscillation check.** outcome-5 (expose fingerprint time) against Story 3 (guard unchanged): satisfying either leaves the other satisfiable, because measurement is returned beside the manifest rather than inside it. Architecture-review condition C-2 (no latency-improvement claim) holds: no criterion or check asserts faster startup.
- **Achievability.** Every criterion's quoted check names its mechanism (`manifest()`, `fingerprintLiveBoundary`, `verifyLiveBoundary`, `EVENT_SINKS`, `renderDaemonEvent`, the conductor emit, `ConductorEventEmitter.emit`) and the observable it produces. Input boundary: Task 6's harness check drives the real `Conductor` with `EventPersister` attached, so the persisted record is produced from the real emission rather than a hand-built event; Task 5's render check takes a constructed event, and Task 6 owns proof that the real event has that shape.
