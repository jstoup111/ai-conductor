# Coherence: Plans declare ordered slices of one feature (#2723)

Date: 2026-09-29
Track: technical
Tier: M
Verdict: covered

## Outcome, story and task mapping

Twelve staged intake outcomes (the five malformation sub-bullets stage as their own outcomes), eight stories, 14 tasks, one changed-ADR row, and 61 criterion rows. Outcome quotes are the staged projection verbatim. No PRD layer applies: this is a technical-track spec. Criterion quotes are exact completion-check text from a cited task's Done when block, and every criterion row passed an independent coverage judgement (61 of 61, three rounds). The consistency pass found one issue and fixed it in DECIDE: the event's `manifest: added` value could never be produced, because a manifest newly added to an unsliced plan is a baseline that Story 7 says emits nothing. It was removed from ADR D6 and Task 10. The preserved-behavior sweep found no new side effect on the unsliced path: land, coverage_binding and discovery all leave unsliced plans unchanged (Tasks 4, 7 and 12).
