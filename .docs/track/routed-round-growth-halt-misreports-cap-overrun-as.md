# Track: A routed round's BUILD cap halt states its real charge, names its work, and reports the enforced growth cap

Track: technical

Scope boundary: The four desired outcomes of intake jstoup111/ai-conductor#3456, which also covers duplicate #3476 (same root defect). Scope was delegated to the composer by the operator. Included: (1) a BUILD-dispatch cap halt raised by a routed round (`settleRoundAtDispatch` returns `exhausted`) states the round's pending growth charge, the cap and settled growth that settlement evaluated, and the minimum raise that admits the round; raising by that amount admits it at the next dispatch. (2) That halt names the round id, the round's finding ids, and the remediation task ids its frozen allocation would add, and it never prints the "repair record is malformed" sentence. (3) When a routed round's settlement cannot read or validate a route record, the halt is `needs-human` (not `kickback-cap`), names the round or ref, and offers no computed raise. (4) `ai-conductor kickback-budget inspect` and `ai-conductor daemon status` derive the config-derived growth cap from the configuration the dispatching engine loads (the main checkout's project config), so they report the same cap as the halt. Excluded: the flat (`pendingRepair`) halt text, which accepted stories pin; inspect and daemon status rendering of a routed round's pending growth, which stays unchanged as `growth-exhausted-build-halt-hides-the-pending-grow` Story 4 requires; config-derived lap allowances on those surfaces; the halt header and halting-gate selection; the cap formula, config keys, raise/reset binding, and the ledger and route-record schemas; the in-process executor (`executor_isolation: in-process`), which loads merged config once at daemon start; any new event, sidecar, or CLI flag.

Internal operator tooling diagnostics with no product requirements. It changes observable halt text, a halt class, and the cap two CLIs report, so it is not a maintenance change. Acceptance criteria live in stories.

Scope check (`.agents/skills/scope-check`): engine behavior that runs in every repository using stacked routed repair. It touches no self-host machinery, rule file, or skill catalog, and it is provider-agnostic.

## Duplicate determination

Both #3456 and #3476 have one root defect. The BUILD halt composer in the engine's dispatch admission (`settleBuildPendingRepair` in `src/conductor/src/engine/conductor.ts`) builds its findings line and its pending-growth view only from the flat ledger's `pendingRepair` and `pendingAsBuiltRemediationFindings`. A routed round carries its charge on the route record's receipt, items, and allocation, and has no flat `pendingRepair`. So every routed cap halt falls through to the "repair record is malformed" sentence and prints no pending growth or minimum raise. #3476's three outcomes (name the round, its findings, and its tasks; state needed versus remaining growth; say "malformed" only on a real read failure) are a subset of #3456's outcomes 1 to 3. #3456 is the primary issue because it was filed first and adds outcome 4, the cap mismatch.

## Approach decision

- **Chosen: settlement returns the round it refused (not derived from the filer's sketch).** `settleRoundAtDispatch` already reads the route record, reconciles receipts, and evaluates the receipt's charge under the ledger lease. Its `exhausted` result gains the round's id, finding ids, allocated task ids, as-built findings, and per-gate charges, built by the same `receiptCharges` that settlement charges, plus the settled growth and cap it evaluated. The halt builds its pending-growth view from those figures through the existing `withPendingGrowth`/`projectPendingGrowth` projection, so the minimum raise printed is the raise that admits the round. A route-record read or reconciliation failure stops being reported as `exhausted` laps and becomes a `halted` result whose reason names the record. The halt already classes `halted` as `needs-human`. Est. effort: about half a day (S). Impact: the routed halt has a single source of truth, and it cannot claim corruption or misstate the raise.
- **Rejected: the halt re-reads the route record itself (filer hypothesis: "a routed-receipt-aware source ... in the same way settleRoundAtDispatch already reads the receipt").** Smaller diff in `round-receipt.ts`. But the halt would re-read the record outside the ledger lease after settlement, and would re-derive the receipt arithmetic. A concurrent write or a reconciliation applied inside settlement could then make the printed figures differ from the ones that refused admission. Est. about 3h.
- **Rejected: a flat `pendingRepair` shadow written for routed rounds.** It reuses the flat rendering unchanged, but it duplicates durable routed state into the ledger, against `adr-2026-10-12-routed-whole-feature-repair` decision 10 (the receipt is the routed charge's single record). Est. about 1 day.
- **Cap agreement: the read-only surfaces follow the enforcer.** The halt's cap comes from the conductor's in-memory config, which the default child executor loads from the main checkout (`loadConfig(projectRoot)` in `executor-child-runtime.ts`, with the conductor's `projectRoot` set to the feature worktree). Inspect and daemon status load the feature worktree's checked-out `.ai-conductor/config.yml` instead. The surfaces are changed to match the enforcer, not the reverse: making the engine read each branch's config would pin every in-flight feature to stale settings, which is a much wider behavior change.

## Verified facts (source on main `7aa1bba459`)

- The halt's findings line falls back to `Pending remediation findings are unavailable because the repair record is malformed.` whenever `settlementLedger.pendingRepair` carries no prd_audit criteria and no task ids. Its growth view is `withPendingGrowth(..., settlementLedger?.pendingRepair)`. Neither reads the route record, so a routed `exhausted` result always gets the malformed sentence and no pending growth (`conductor.ts` lines 10355-10421). Basis: verified.
- `settleRoundAtDispatch` returns `{ kind: 'exhausted', gate: 'prd_audit', allowance: 'laps' }` both when the current route record is not `record` and when receipt reconciliation reports `malformed`/`unreadable` (`routing/round-receipt.ts` lines 279-297). The conductor treats any `exhausted` result as a `kickback-cap` halt. Basis: verified.
- `adr-2026-10-12-routed-whole-feature-repair` decision 10 says a malformed route record "counts as an exhausted budget, as the ledger does". `adr-2026-08-31-kickback-ledger-read-fails-closed` decision 1 routes an unvalidatable ledger to the `needs-human` class. A `needs-human` routed record halt therefore conforms to both ADRs and needs no amendment. Basis: verified.
- The routed growth refusal is `prior.added + receiptCharges(record).growth > (effectiveGrowthCap ?? budgets[0].growthCap)`. That growth is attributed to the first charged gate in `WHOLE_FEATURE_BUDGET_GATES` order. `kickback-budget raise` for a growth halt sets `effectiveGrowthCap = (effectiveGrowthCap ?? capEvidence.limit) + by`, and the halt records the same cap as `capEvidence.limit`. So `added + growth − cap` is the minimum admitting raise (`round-receipt.ts` lines 160-245, `kickback-budget-cli.ts` lines 343-356). Basis: verified.
- Cap divergence: the default `executor_isolation: child` executor loads config from the main checkout, while `kickback-budget inspect` (`planGrowthViewFor`) and `daemon status` load it from the feature worktree. On 2026-10-26 main lowered `prd_audit.max_appended_tasks` from 10 to 8 (#3443, 13:07Z). The feature branches `feat/c1/build-review-re-raises-already-decided-findings-ev` and `feat/c1/manual-reviewer-edits-cannot-be-landed-safely-in-t` kept 10 until restacks on 2026-10-26 22:20 and 2026-10-27 17:22 (reflogs). That matches the build-review halt (cap 8) disagreeing with inspect (cap 10). Basis: verified (mechanism); inferred (90%) that this is the observed instance.
