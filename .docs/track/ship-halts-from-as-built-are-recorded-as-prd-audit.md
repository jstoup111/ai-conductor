# Track: SHIP halts from as-built are recorded as prd_audit, hiding them in metrics (#3287)

Track: technical

Scope boundary: Balanced, bounded by #3287's four desired outcomes. Every terminal halt the daemon validation group raises names one attributed member step instead of the group's entry step, and that same step is carried by the `loop_halt` event, by the committed halt record's `Halting step:` line, and by the halted `feature_dispatch_ended` event. Attribution is engine-assigned at the halt site, never derived from halt text: a member's own dispatch fault (no-verdict, mechanical fault, permission denial, authentication recovery) is attributed to that member; every prd_audit route halt (projection, plan gap, over-scope, refusal rework) to `prd_audit`; every as-built halt (verdict, projection refusal, no-op escalation, remediation halt) to `architecture_review_as_built`; a gap member's kickback no-op to that gap member; a remediation-planner halt to the first non-manual_test gap member it was planning; and the generic multi-member halt to the first failing member in group order, as it already does for its refusal. When more than one member fails, every failing member is recorded through the existing per-member group refusal on the spine, so the remediation-planner halts that skip that record today gain it. Under the default child-executor isolation the supervisor-emitted halted `feature_dispatch_ended` currently carries no `step` and no `haltClass` at all; the child's terminal outcome carries both to the supervisor so the event and the OTel halt metric are restored. Excluded: changing halt reason text, renaming the group or its entry step, the halted event's `tier` field and `feature_dispatch_started` parity in child mode, back-filling existing halt records or events, the serial (interactive) SHIP path whose dispatching step is already the member, and #2060's shared remediation budget.

Internal engine telemetry correctness — no user-facing product capability; acceptance criteria come from #3287's desired outcomes and live directly in stories. Changes observable event and record content, so not a maintenance change.

Approach: explicit member attribution at each group halt site (selected) over deriving the step afterwards from member events on the spine (not every halt path emits a member event, and the halt record is written before them) and over reclassifying by halt text in metric consumers (string matching on prose; the issue's own measurement is what this fix must make unnecessary).
