The observable leak is real; its source is the test suite. The issue's third desired outcome ("a process audit finds no keepalive whose session is gone") is therefore delivered at its true source, and the first two, which name a per-session keepalive mechanism that does not exist, are recorded here as not implementable rather than invented.
