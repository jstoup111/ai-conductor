# Track: Restack resolver accepts an operator decision

Track: technical

Scope boundary: balanced. Stacked restack conflict halts of both causes, feature-repair and base-refresh. In scope: (1) an operator decision recorded with `ai-conductor stack restack-resolve --decision "<answer>"`, the sibling of #3522's `--tip` on the same command, bound to the conflict it answers (child, range, replayed commit, paths), delivered to the next restack resolver attempt for that conflict, and recorded and applied as event-spine occurrences; (2) #3522's conflict record, `Restack-Conflict` halt line and recipe extended from feature-repair to base-refresh conflicts, so both `--tip` and `--decision` work for either cause; (3) the stacked-recovery runbook documents the decide-then-resume path, including for a closed child. A conflict with no decision still escalates as today. Excluded: plain (non-stacked) rebase, CI-fix and setup resolvers; merge-bearing `flatten_refused` halts; the stale `HALT.cleared` body and the daemon clear-watcher's attribution that reads it, split to jstoup111/ai-conductor#3551 on 2026-10-31 because they belong to daemon wake and the prd_audit widening carrier, not restack.

Engine and operator tooling with no end-user product requirements; acceptance criteria live in stories. Operator confirmed 2026-10-31 after #3421 landed as #3522. Chosen approach: `stack restack-resolve --decision`, which shares the command's lease, its check that the record matches the live halt, and its HALT clear, and needs no TTY. Rejected: `halt clear --decision`, the 2026-10-29 pick, because it is TTY-only and would give one halt kind two clearing commands with different rules; a deterministic `--prefer upstream|source` side-pick, because it cannot express a semantic answer.
