# Track: Remediation-gate budget readable from one place that agrees with enforcement

Track: technical

Scope boundary: Read-side only. The authoritative, operator-facing reading of a feature's remediation budget for `prd_audit` and `architecture_review_as_built` is the shared kickback-budget projection, and both of its operator surfaces must agree with what the engine enforces: `ai-conductor kickback-budget inspect` (adds the not-yet-charged pending-repair lap and resolves the fallback cap through the engine's own resolver) and `ai-conductor daemon status` (renders both remediation gates for every in-progress or halted feature before any halt, unavailable when the durable entry is unreadable). Excluded: any change to how laps are charged, settled, capped, raised, or reset; the `kickback-ledger.json` schema and its `count`/`cumulative` semantics; cap evidence and second-raise recovery (#2894); tier-derived or redesigned budgets (#2413); the process-local `remediationRounds` dispatch counter; child-ledger rendering in daemon status.

Root cause (verified, 90%): the remediation lap cap reads `gates.<gate>.laps` (`src/conductor/src/engine/remediation-caps.ts:117-121`; at filing time the halt text `lap cap reached (${priorLaps}/${lapCap})` read the same field), while `count`/`cumulative` are the generic re-open counters bumped only by `bumpKickbackGate` (`src/conductor/src/engine/kickback-ledger.ts:1007-1045`) and never by a remediation lap. The issue's reader printed the wrong fields of the right file. The projection that already reads the right field (`kickback-budget-view.ts:62-64`) is the place to make authoritative, rather than stamping derived budget into durable state (it would drift with config) or renaming persisted counters (a schema migration overlapping #2413). Internal operator tooling; acceptance criteria live in stories (intake jstoup111/ai-conductor#2577).
