# Intake origin: qualify-the-reported-cost-figure-when-it-covers-on

Source-Ref: jstoup111/ai-conductor#1863
Owner: jstoup111

## Desired outcome
- When some dispatches contributed no cost, the reported money figure is legible as covering
  only the dispatches it was summed over — a reader cannot mistake it for the whole run's cost.
- When every dispatch was measured, the line reads as a plain total with no added qualification
  or ceremony.
- No dispatch acquires a fabricated, estimated, or extrapolated cost in order to make the
  denominators agree.
- The existing exclusion counts remain visible and keep their present meanings.
